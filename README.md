### 📌 Flagship Projects

#### 🌧️ [LankaFlood-AI: National Flood & Climate Risk Engine](https://github.com/rasindupramith-oss/sri-lanka-flood-risk-engine)
* **What it does:** Calibrated decision-support engine applying **Extreme Value Theory (Gumbel distributions)** and soil saturation physics across all 25 districts of Sri Lanka (2015–2024, 91,325 days).
* **Validation:** Autonomously validated against **May 2016 Cyclone Roanu** (>99% critical alert in Gampaha/Kegalle).
* 🔗 **[Live Interactive Web App](https://sri-lanka-flood-risk-engine.streamlit.app/)**

#### 🚚 [Western Province Logistics & Route Optimization](https://github.com/rasindupramith-oss/western-province-logistics-route-optimizer)
* **What it does:** Solved the Traveling Salesperson Problem (TSP) across major supply nodes in Sri Lanka's Western Province.
* **Impact:** Reduced transit mileage by **41%**, saving an estimated **Rs. 15.4M annually** in fleet fuel and operational expenditure.

#### 💵 [Sri Lanka Salary, APIT Tax & Take-Home Pay Simulator](https://github.com/rasindupramith-oss/sri-lanka-salary-tax-simulator)
* **What it does:** Real-time financial intelligence simulator calibrated with Inland Revenue Department (IRD) progressive tax brackets and EPF/ETF deductions.
* 🔗 **[Live Interactive Web App](https://sri-lanka-salary-tax-simulator.streamlit.app/)**

#### 🏏 [Moneyball Sri Lanka: Integer Linear Optimization](https://github.com/rasindupramith-oss/sri-lanka-cricket-analytics-moneyball)
* **What it does:** Applied **Integer Linear Programming (ILP)** and player impact metrics on historical ball-by-ball data to mathematically select the optimal All-Time Greatest Sri Lanka T20 XI under strict team constraints.

#### 📈 [Financial Time Series Forecasting (Gold Futures)](https://github.com/rasindupramith-oss/gold-price-forecasting-arima)
* **What it does:** Rigorous econometric modeling using Box-Jenkins ARIMA methodology with out-of-sample rolling evaluation, achieving an **8.26% MAPE**.
